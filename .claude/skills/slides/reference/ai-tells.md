# AI tells to avoid on slides

Source: Wikipedia, "Signs of AI writing" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) plus our own deck reviews (2026-10-04). `lint_deck.py` flags the words in `AI_TELLS`.

**Words:** crucial, pivotal, seamless, leverage, unlock, empower, robust, game-changer, cutting-edge, delve, landscape, tapestry, underscore, foster, elevate, streamline, transform, journey, harness, effortless, end-to-end, at scale.
**Patterns:** "not just X, but Y"; "serves as / stands as" instead of "is"; -ing tails ("…, ensuring accuracy"); vague attribution ("experts say"); significance inflation ("a pivotal moment").
**Slide design tells:** tracked uppercase eyebrow above every title; three equal cards with icons; a giant gradient number; every slide a triad; bold scattered through sentences; em dashes; punchy two-word fragments as titles ("Watch it run.", "One take, no cuts"); emoji.
**Fabricated precision:** numbers produced from synthetic data shown as results. Say what the program does instead.
**Do instead:** plain verbs (is, does, checks, writes), the partner's own words, one specific claim per slide, sentence case, short.
