Definitions

Revenue
Sales minus refunds, before marketplace fees; excludes shipping and tax (as in the nightly pulse).
Period
Calendar month, Eastern time.
Customers
Sum of the daily counts, so a buyer who orders on two days counts twice.
Gross margin (est.)
(Revenue - orders x simulated cost per order) / revenue.
Sell-through
Orders / simulated new listings in the period (a proxy until listing ids are joined).
Category revenue
