# Demo script 2 of 3: Manager view (COO Scorecards)

Presenter: Orlando. About 45 to 55 seconds. Same server, nothing typed. Replaces the old `demo_script_scorecard.md` for the new three-part take.

## Before you record
1. Same setup as script 1. `python -m engine.store status --month 2026-09` should say 30 of 30 days loaded.
2. Start from the page you finished script 1 on (or the Overview).

## 1. Pick the period (about 15 s)
**Do:** top bar, **COO Scorecards**.

> "For managers and the COO: the same data, rolled up. Here you can move through the months, the weeks, even single days, and the fifteen KPIs for any of them."

**Do:** point at the **KPI Trend** picker on this list page. Choose an area (Financial, say), then switch **Days, Weeks, Months**.

> "Pick an area and a period, and the same fifteen KPIs redraw from the same database."

## 2. Open September (about 30 s)
**Do:** click **September 2026** in the Months rows.

**Point at, in order:**
1. The headline: total e-commerce revenue and its change versus August.
2. **Revenue by Marketplace** (the pie): ShopGoodwill, eBay, Amazon.
3. The five areas: Financial, Productivity, Inventory, Sales, Category + Customer. Click one **(i)** button to show the definition.
4. A tile with the **Simulated internal data** badge, then one marked **Partial data**.

> "These are Goodwill's own fifteen KPIs, in their five areas. Eleven of them need internal data we have not seen, labor hours, listings, cost of goods, so they run on a mock and every one says so on the tile. And where data is incomplete, like buyer ids from Amazon, it says partial instead of pretending."

**Do:** scroll to the bottom and show the **KPI Table (CSV)** and **PDF** buttons.

> "The same numbers for Excel, Power BI, or as a one-page PDF."

## Say plainly if asked
- August comes from the simulators, so "down 33%" only shows the comparison works. It is not a trend.
- KPI definitions are our reading of Goodwill's slides, not confirmed by them.
